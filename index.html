app.ly privacy policy

app.ly stores your profile, learned answers, drafts and any AI provider API keys locally in your browser. This data is not sent to the app.ly developer or stored on an app.ly server.

The extension reads the form on the page you choose to fill and fills it from your saved profile. Page contents are not recorded or transmitted to the developer.

If you use the AI assistant, the text needed for your request (for example parts of your profile or a job description) is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you configured, using your own API key, under that provider's terms.

If you use the optional account sign-in, your Google account identity is used to connect your browser to a relay that passes requests from your MCP app to your browser. The relay stores nothing.

app.ly never submits an application, sends a message or email, or presses a payment button without your action. It does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it for credit or lending decisions. You can export or delete all data from the extension at any time.
